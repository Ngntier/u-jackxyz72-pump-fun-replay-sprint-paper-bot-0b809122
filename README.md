A small-stake (0.02 SOL) launch sniper for the pump.fun replay: it buys only young launches that survived their first seconds with buyers still stepping in, and exits fast — half at +10%, the rest at +25%, everything at −6%, on a creator sell, or after 50 slots. At most 5 open positions, no shorts, no leverage. Fully deterministic (bigint money math, no clock or randomness). Paper money only — no live funds. Free to enter.
